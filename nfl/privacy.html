Privacy policy

Pro Football Board

Effective November 15, 2026. Applies to the pro football board at cfbgameday.app/nfl and the Pro Football Board app for iPhone and iPad, both published by Hoyne Labs LLC.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or notifications, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your device

The teams you follow and the last copy of the scoreboard are stored on your device so the app can open quickly and show something when you are offline. They never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

The widgets download the same public scores the app shows and keep the last copy on your device. They send no identifiers.

Network requests

The app requests public data from cfbgameday.app, which in turn fetches ESPN's public scoreboard and game feed, nflverse season metrics and Open-Meteo weather. Your device talks only to cfbgameday.app. Your requests carry no account or personal data.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps standard access logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for a short period for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or profile anyone. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Links

Links to ESPN and other sites open in Safari under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app shows scores, schedules and statistics only. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: hoynelabs@gmail.com.
