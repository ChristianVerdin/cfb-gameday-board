Pro Football Board

A one-screen pro football slate: kickoff times in US Central, TV, venue and kickoff-hour weather, then live score, clock, drives, win probability and season metrics once games start.

On iPhone: Pro Football Board on the App Store, with Home Screen widgets.

Contact

Missing game, wrong score, a crash, or the app will not load: email hoynelabs@gmail.com. Include the game and the time you saw the problem.

FAQ

Where does the data come from?

Scores and play data come from ESPN's public feed. Season metrics come from nflverse (CC BY 4.0). Weather comes from Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Everything passes through cfbgameday.app, so the app itself needs no account.

How often does it refresh?

Live games update about every 30 seconds while the app is open. The weekly slate, venues and kickoff-hour weather are rebuilt a few times a week, and the season metrics refresh weekly. Widgets refresh on a schedule iOS controls, so they can trail the app by a few minutes.

What is the win probability?

It is ESPN's published win probability for the game, charted through the game. It is a statistic, not a prediction made by this app.

Why is a game missing or shown as TBD?

Flexed games and playoff matchups appear once the league and ESPN publish them.
