CFB GameDay Board

A one-screen college football slate: venue, kickoff-hour weather, TV and streaming, publicly posted lines and implied scores, then live score, clock, and cover/total state once games start. Times are shown in US Central.

What it is not

It is not a sportsbook and it does not accept wagers, deposits, or payments. Lines are the DraftKings numbers ESPN publishes with its scoreboard, shown for context. Nothing here is betting advice.

How the numbers work

  • proj is the implied score from the posted spread and total. It is a projection, not points scored.
  • HOME COVER / AWAY COVER compares the current margin with the posted home spread. need X for OVER is the total minus the combined score.
  • Weather is the Open-Meteo forecast for the venue city at the kickoff hour, captured when the week's slate was built, and refreshed Saturday morning.
  • Live data updates every 30 seconds from ESPN's public scoreboard.

Something wrong?

Missing game, bad line, wrong venue, or the app will not load: open an issue at github.com/ChristianVerdin/cfb-gameday-board/issues. Include the game and the time you saw the problem.
