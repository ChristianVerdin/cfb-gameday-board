This is CFB GameDay Board, launched from the home screen on an iPhone.
The Board tab shows every FBS game this week. Each card has the venue, the kickoff time in Central, the kickoff-hour weather at the stadium, the TV network, and the publicly posted line with the implied score.
Day and conference filters narrow the slate, and search finds a team, stadium, city, or network.
Tapping a venue opens Apple Maps outside the app.
Pull down to refresh.
The Live tab lists games in progress with the score, the clock, and whether the favorite is covering. Between game days it shows the latest finals.
Starring a game adds it to the Starred tab.
About lists the data sources, ESPN and Open-Meteo, with links to the privacy policy and the support page.
With no connection, the app shows an offline view and a Retry button.
There are no accounts, no purchases, no ads, and no wagering. It is an information display.
